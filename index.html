      Questions people ask

      Do you charge a call-out fee?

      Yes, $79 for the trip and diagnosis. If you go ahead with the repair, it's credited to the bill. Nights, Sundays and holidays add a flat $125 after-hours fee, which we tell you on the phone before we drive.

      How fast can you get here?

      For emergencies inside our area, usually within an hour or two. For everything else, the board above shows the next open window, often the same day. You get a text with your tech's name when they're on the way.

      Do you do remodels?

      No. We do service calls, replacements and installs: fixing a leak, swapping a water heater, adding an outlet, replacing a panel. For a new bathroom or an addition you need a general contractor, and we're happy to suggest one.

      Do you work on wells or septic systems?

      Partly. We work on the house side of a well: pressure tanks, pressure switches and the wiring to the pump. We don't drill wells, pull pumps or pump septic tanks, but we'll point you to people who do.

      Is the price on the site the price I pay?

      The site shows honest ranges. The tech confirms the exact price in writing after looking, before any work starts. If it's more than you want to spend, you only pay the trip fee.
